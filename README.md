# CASEVO AI — Sourcing MVP

This is the first front-end MVP for CASEVO's AI-powered China sourcing platform.

## Current MVP
- Professional English B2B positioning
- China-inspired visual language
- AI Sourcing intake form
- Structured sourcing brief
- Illustrative supplier-match results
- CASEVO Score™ concept
- Supplier Intelligence Report product
- Sourcing project lead form

## Important
The supplier matches shown in this MVP are explicitly demo/illustrative data. No supplier should be represented as verified until CASEVO completes real research and human verification.
